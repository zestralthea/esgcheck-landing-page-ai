# Positioning

ESGCheck is a practical **ESG evidence and response platform** for B2B SMEs and suppliers facing rising ESG information requests from customers, procurement teams, lenders, investors, and supply-chain partners.

The product helps companies understand what an ESG request is asking, find the relevant evidence they already have, identify what is missing, and prepare a credible evidence-backed response for human review. It is built from Switzerland, with Swiss/EU trust expectations and privacy-conscious data handling as core positioning pillars.

## Category

ESG evidence and response platform

## Core customer promise

ESGCheck helps B2B SMEs and suppliers prepare credible answers to ESG information requests by translating ESG language into practical information needs, finding supporting evidence across existing company materials, identifying gaps, and preparing traceable responses for human approval.

# Mission

ESGCheck helps B2B SMEs and suppliers understand what ESG information is being requested, what they can already demonstrate, what evidence is missing, and how developed their underlying ESG practices are.

The mission is to lower the barrier to credible ESG work for smaller companies by making evidence collection, response preparation, and ESG guidance more practical, more structured, and less dependent on heavy consulting or enterprise software from the start.

# Long-Term Vision

ESGCheck aims to become a trusted ESG evidence and response workflow for growing SMEs in Switzerland and, over time, across Europe.

It begins with practical support for ESG questions and information requests and is intended to evolve into a reusable company ESG knowledge layer in which evidence, company facts, approvals, and prior answers can support future requests where they remain current and applicable.

# Market Need

Sustainability expectations are rising, shaped by EU sustainability reporting frameworks such as CSRD and ESRS, Swiss non-financial reporting rules for larger companies, and broader market expectations around responsible business conduct. For many SMEs, the most immediate effect is not direct regulation, but indirect pressure from larger customers, procurement teams, supply-chain partners, banks, investors, and financial institutions.

These stakeholders increasingly ask smaller companies for ESG information, supporting evidence, policies, operational data, or questionnaires. The practical challenge is often that relevant information is scattered across Finance, HR, Operations, Procurement, Management, invoices, spreadsheets, policies, and internal records, while non-ESG specialists may not know what the request means or what counts as sufficient evidence.

VSME provides a practical SME-oriented backbone for this target group. GRI remains a complementary methodology and reference layer, while ESRS provides CSRD-related context where relevant. The actual customer, procurement, lender, or stakeholder request remains the immediate business task ESGCheck helps the SME solve.

Traditional ESG consulting and enterprise sustainability platforms are often too expensive, too manual, or too operationally complex for smaller organizations. This creates a market gap for practical tools that help SMEs understand ESG requests, organize evidence, identify gaps, and prepare credible responses without the overhead of full consulting engagements or heavyweight software.

# Business Relevance Beyond Compliance

ESGCheck is not only about helping companies keep up with regulation. ESG increasingly affects how companies are perceived by customers, partners, investors, lenders, and larger organizations in their ecosystem.

For many growing SMEs, the immediate business value is the ability to respond to an important external request with greater speed, confidence, and credibility. A company that can find and substantiate its ESG information is often in a stronger position to:

- respond to customer and procurement expectations more confidently
- strengthen trust with customers and partners
- demonstrate maturity to investors, lenders, and larger counterparties
- reuse approved ESG information across future requests
- identify internal gaps before they become urgent or externally visible problems

ESGCheck is therefore intended to serve as both a practical response tool and a reusable evidence layer that makes ESG more visible, actionable, and worthwhile for SMEs to engage with meaningfully.

# Product Today

ESGCheck has a **functioning technical prototype and is currently in a product repositioning and MVP definition phase**. The existing prototype already connects company setup, document upload, AI extraction, ESG metrics, source traceability, and basic GRI report generation. The revised MVP is now being defined around ESG requests, evidence, reusable company context, and human-reviewed responses.

The product has not yet undergone external user validation, and the revised workflow has not yet received its detailed implementation go signal.

## Current practical output

For the revised MVP, ESGCheck is intended to provide:

- evidence mapping from available company information
- question-level statuses: Supported, Partially Supported, Missing Evidence, and Potentially Not Applicable
- gap identification and practical next steps
- **Evidence Readiness**, showing how complete and usable the available evidence base is
- **ESG Maturity**, included in the first MVP as a qualitative view of how developed the company's actual ESG practices, processes, controls, and management systems appear to be
- evidence-backed suggested responses for human review where sufficient support exists

More evidence should improve ESGCheck's ability to assess the company; it must not automatically improve ESG Maturity. **TO BE DISCUSSED:** The exact presentation of ESG Maturity and the first sellable output remain open.

## Current framework foundation

- VSME as the primary SME methodological backbone
- GRI as a complementary methodology and reference layer
- ESRS for CSRD-related context and mapping where relevant
- customer, procurement, lender, or other stakeholder questionnaires as the specific business-request layer
- SASB or other sector guidance where useful and validated

The SME should not need to understand or actively select this framework hierarchy before receiving practical help.

## Current input logic

The revised direction starts from the real ESG problem rather than requiring the SME to begin with an ESG framework or a predefined document list. A user may bring a single ESG question, a questionnaire, another sustainability request, and/or existing company evidence.

ESGCheck should identify what information is required, search what the company already has, and guide the user toward what is still missing. **TO BE DISCUSSED:** The minimum company context required before analysis has not yet been finalized.

# Workflow and Document Readiness

At a high level, ESGCheck is intended to follow a simple evidence-and-response workflow:

1. A company brings a real ESG question, questionnaire, or stakeholder request.
2. ESGCheck explains what is being asked and identifies the information and evidence required.
3. The system searches existing evidence and previously approved company information.
4. Each question is classified as Supported, Partially Supported, Missing Evidence, or Potentially Not Applicable.
5. For unresolved items, ESGCheck explains what is missing and where it may be found.
6. ESGCheck prepares an evidence-backed answer once sufficient information is available.
7. A human approves the answer; approved evidence and facts can be reused while still current.

ESGCheck should search for **evidence and information**, not rigidly named documents. The same fact may be supported by different credible sources depending on the company. A missing document title should therefore not automatically be treated as missing ESG information.

Evidence Readiness and ESG Maturity must remain separate. Finding an electricity bill does not prove that Scope 2 emissions have been calculated; finding a policy does not automatically prove that the underlying practice has been implemented. More documents increase the system's ability to assess the company, not the company's ESG performance itself.

**TO BE DISCUSSED:** The exact end-to-end sequence, mandatory company-context fields, whether questionnaires become the primary MVP entry point, and the role of report generation still require team agreement.

# Technical and Data Foundation

ESGCheck is built around an AI-assisted evidence workflow designed to make ESG work more practical and scalable for smaller companies.

The most important technical messages for strategic and external discussions are:

- the functioning prototype already supports company setup, document upload, AI extraction, ESG metrics, source traceability, gap detection, and basic GRI report generation
- the revised product should organize work around requests, evidence, validation status, human approval, and reusable responses
- important claims and approved company facts should remain traceable and reusable where still valid
- privacy-conscious data handling and strong customer-data separation are core design priorities

The detailed technical stack should remain outside this strategic master document and be maintained separately in technical documentation or a software fact sheet.

# Trust, Privacy, and Credibility Boundaries

Privacy is one of ESGCheck's major strategic requirements because ESG-related company material can be commercially sensitive, incomplete, operationally revealing, and reputationally important.

ESGCheck is being designed toward strong customer-data separation, controlled access, traceable evidence handling, responsible use of external AI providers, clear retention and deletion practices, and defensible Swiss/EU-oriented privacy claims. The team intends to validate the architecture, provider behavior, hosting, retention, data-processing questions, and external privacy claims with appropriate legal and privacy expertise.

Human approval is non-negotiable. The operating principle is: **AI proposes. Evidence supports. Humans approve. The company owns.**

At its current stage, ESGCheck is not:

- a substitute for formal assurance or professional legal/audit work
- a legal certification mechanism
- a full CSRD compliance suite
- a guarantee of ESG performance
- an enterprise platform for complex multi-subsidiary consolidation or large-scale supplier-survey distribution

Commercial credibility depends on disciplined claims. ESGCheck should win by being focused, traceable, useful, and transparent about uncertainty rather than by presenting AI output as automatically authoritative.

# Go-to-Market Logic

The strongest first commercial wedge for ESGCheck remains B2B SMEs and suppliers that are asked to provide ESG-related information by larger customers, procurement teams, supply-chain partners, lenders, investors, or other important stakeholders.

This wedge is strongest because the customer arrives with a real business trigger, often a stakeholder and a deadline. Instead of asking the SME to care about ESG in the abstract, ESGCheck can address a concrete need such as: "A customer sent me this ESG questionnaire and I need to answer it credibly."

This wedge combines:

- real and growing external pressure
- a concrete reason to gather and organize evidence
- measurable time and coordination savings
- a clear definition of success
- a credible basis for later reuse of the company's ESG evidence

# Product Differentiation

ESGCheck should not primarily compete by sounding like another generic sustainability software product.

Its differentiation is strongest in the following combination:

1. **Evidence and response focus:** start from the real business request and turn it into a practical response workflow.
2. **Built for SMEs:** designed for companies without dedicated ESG teams or enterprise-level resources.
3. **Evidence-backed and traceable:** search for the information required to support a claim, keep answers linked to evidence, and separate Evidence Readiness from ESG Maturity.
4. **Human approval and reuse:** external answers require human approval, while approved evidence and facts can support future requests where still valid.
5. **Privacy-conscious:** Swiss/EU-oriented trust expectations and disciplined privacy claims remain central.

# Validation and Roadmap

From an accelerator, board, or expert perspective, ESGCheck should currently be understood as a promising but still validation-stage company with a functioning technical prototype and a clearer product hypothesis under definition.

The most important near-term validation goals are:

- demonstrate that SMEs have a concrete external trigger to use the product
- show that ESGCheck understands requests, retrieves relevant evidence, and produces useful statuses
- confirm that Evidence Readiness and qualitative ESG Maturity are understood as different concepts
- confirm that users trust the evidence-backed, human-reviewed outputs enough to act on them
- validate willingness to pay once the first sellable output is defined

The immediate roadmap is:

1. Agree the revised end-to-end workflow and resolve the remaining first-feedback questions.
2. Define minimum company context, the first sellable output, and the exact MVP boundary.
3. Implement the agreed changes while reusing the functioning prototype where appropriate.
4. Test with deliberately different fictional companies, realistic files, and cross-LLM comparison where useful.
5. Review privacy, hosting, AI-provider, retention, and compliance questions with appropriate legal/privacy expertise.
6. Prepare for controlled external pilot validation and refine positioning, pricing, and service structure from evidence.

A rough four-to-six-month technical horizon was discussed as a feasible planning range, not as a rigid deadline.

**TO BE DISCUSSED:** Questionnaire priority, report-generation priority, certification ambition, geographic focus, pricing, and the first sellable output remain open decisions.

If ESGCheck were being evaluated by a startup accelerator, innovation board, or early-stage support program, the strongest current conclusion would be:

**ESGCheck appears to be a serious and credible early-stage B2B software opportunity with a functioning technical prototype, a clear SME pain point, and a stronger evidence-and-response product hypothesis, but it still requires workflow, market, trust, and willingness-to-pay validation before stronger growth claims can be made.**

The reasons it is attractive are:

- the underlying market pressure is real
- the SME pain point and evidence-and-response wedge are understandable
- the functioning prototype reduces pure technical-concept risk
- human approval and source traceability provide a credible trust model
- VSME-first methodology and reusable evidence fit the intended SME use case

The main risks are:

- trust risk if evidence matching or suggested answers feel shallow or overconfident
- methodology risk if Evidence Readiness and ESG Maturity are not clearly separated
- sales risk until the first sellable output and willingness to pay are validated
- workflow risk while company context, questionnaire priority, reports, and user roles remain under discussion
- privacy and regulatory-positioning risk if external claims exceed what the architecture, provider contracts, or methodology can prove

This makes ESGCheck a project that is strong enough to deserve serious pilot-stage backing and ecosystem support, while still being early enough that disciplined validation matters more than broad ambition claims.
